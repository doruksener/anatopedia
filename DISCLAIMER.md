# Medical disclaimer

Anatopedia is an educational software project. It is not a medical device and
must not be used to diagnose, treat, prevent, monitor, or make decisions about
a disease or injury.

The default anatomy classifications are generated from structure names and may
be incomplete or incorrect. Clinical content should not be published without
qualified expert review, source citations, and an appropriate regulatory and
privacy assessment.

Do not upload or commit identifiable patient information. Use only data you
are authorized to process and that has been properly de-identified.
